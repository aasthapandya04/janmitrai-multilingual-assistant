How to Use JanMitra AI

Step-by-step guide to making the most of our AI assistant

I

Open the AI Assistant

Navigate to the Assistant page from the top navigation. Select "Services" to choose what kind of help you need first — Complaint, Form Help, Policy, or Support Routing.

II

Gemini API Key is Stored Securely

This app now uses a server-side Gemini key proxy. Your API key is kept on the backend and is never stored in browser local storage.

III

Describe Your Problem

Type your complaint naturally in Hindi, Hinglish, or English. Use the microphone button for voice input if preferred. You can also click example queries in the sidebar to get started.

IV

Review the AI Response

Gemini AI will identify the department, assess priority, and generate a professional bilingual complaint draft. For complaint filings, a unique ticket ID is assigned automatically.

V

Download & Submit

View your full results on the Results page. Download English and Hindi PDFs, copy the ticket ID, and submit the document to the appropriate government department portal or office.
